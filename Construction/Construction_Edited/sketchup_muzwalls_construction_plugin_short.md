## Plugin facts (checked 28 Oct 2026; not in the audio)

Sources: the video description, <https://muzwalls.com>, <https://muzwalls.com/faq>, and the SketchUp forum thread "MUZWalls Plugin".

- **Name / maker:** MUZWalls, by ShiftDev (shiftdev.de).
- **Where from:** the vendor's site (`/download`, an `.rbz` file) and a SketchUp Extension Warehouse listing.
- **Price:** paid. Monthly $14.99, yearly $119, or perpetual $299 (launch price) plus $79/year for optional updates. Free trial with no card (the site says 14 days; the forum thread says 7; confirm on the page before relying on either). Without a licence key the plugin "keeps showing the free tier."
- **SketchUp versions:** the FAQ says "SketchUp Pro 2021 and later, on both Windows and macOS", and "not compatible with SketchUp Free, SketchUp Go, or the web version."
- **Licence:** the key is pasted under Manage License and "Activation needs a connection."
- **Fit for our SketchUp:** Jonathan's SketchUp is **Make 2017**, so MUZWalls will not load there (2021+ Pro only). It is recorded here as a reference for what a parametric stair/railing/framing plugin does, not as an installed tool. Our own stair generator stays in `ada-stair-generator`.
