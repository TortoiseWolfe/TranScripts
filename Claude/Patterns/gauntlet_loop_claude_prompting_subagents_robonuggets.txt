[4:13] But just to step back in case you don't know what we're referring to when we talk about sub agent orchestration: essentially, when you prompt an agent or talk to an AI model, there's three levels to it, at least in how I think about it. The first level, the most basic and most common, is when you do work with an agent — you send a prompt, it provides an output, you verify if that output matches your standard, and then you send another prompt until you get what you want. But it turns out this role of being the verifier can actually be offloaded to an agent as well. So this concept of loops came about where, at the next level, you can have an agent work for you and also do the verification — this agent assumes the role of a critic, and you just have these two AI agents talk to each other until it meets a certain standard, a bar that you set. Only then will the critic agent pass you the final output.
